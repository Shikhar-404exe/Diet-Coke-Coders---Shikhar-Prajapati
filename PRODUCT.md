# Campus Triage Agent

## Register
product

## Platform
web

## Users
- **Primary — Students:** Need fast, trustworthy answers on Wi‑Fi, portal, hostel, fees, scholarships; want to track their own tickets and confirm before staff is notified.
- **Secondary — Academic / Ops staff:** Claim escalations, meet SLAs, leave notes, reply to students, maintain approved policy PDFs.

## Purpose
A **Track 2 intelligent campus agent**: retrieve from approved documents, refuse when uncertain or off-topic, collect missing slots, and hand off unresolved issues to the right human team — transparent and controllable, not a generic chatbot.

## Positioning
Two doors, one triage brain. Students get calm Help; staff get an ops console. Role is earned at login — never toggled in-app.

## Brand personality
Clear · Trustworthy · Campus-practical

## Anti-references
- SaaS “AI dashboard” purple glow kits
- Staff/student role toggles that anyone can flip
- Dense admin chrome shoved into a student chat
- Generic Inter/Roboto template looks
- Ungrounded “confident” LLM answers with no source

## Accessibility
WCAG AA contrast minimum; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; focus-visible rings on all controls; no color-only status.
